# Private / Proprietary System Directive

## Status

**PRIVATE. PROPRIETARY. COMPANY-BOUND.**

This repository contains the private research and development system for an Institutional Intelligence Engine intended to become a proprietary company-connected decision-intelligence platform.

This directive is explicit and persistent. It applies to future development, agents, contributors, prompts, architectural decisions, deployment decisions, and integrations.

## Core Instruction

> **Treat this system as proprietary intellectual property. Keep the repository private. Do not publish, open-source, expose, or redistribute the system, source code, proprietary architecture, prompts, governance logic, financial models, decision logic, or internal research unless explicitly authorized by the human owner.**

The system is being developed as a private foundation that may eventually be connected to a company and its institutional workflows.

Development and preview environments must not be treated as authorization for production deployment or company integration.

## Future Company Connection

The long-term objective is to make this system capable of serving as a controlled institutional intelligence layer for a company.

When company integration becomes appropriate, it must be treated as a separate authorization and deployment stage, not as an automatic consequence of development progress.

Before connecting company systems, the implementation must establish explicit boundaries for:

- identity and access control
- roles and permissions
- company data access
- proprietary data handling
- secrets and credentials
- auditability
- model and tool permissions
- human authorization
- financial decision boundaries
- external actions
- data retention
- logging
- incident response
- deployment isolation
- intellectual-property protection

No company connection grants the system autonomous authority.

## Human Authority

The human owner retains authority over whether, when, and how the system is:

1. developed
2. deployed
3. connected to company infrastructure
4. granted access to company data
5. granted access to external tools
6. authorized for consequential actions

The system may analyze, research, calculate, simulate, challenge, monitor, and recommend.

It does not acquire authority merely because a technical integration makes an action possible.

## No Autonomous Capital Authority

This system is decision intelligence, not an autonomous investment or trading authority.

It must not independently:

- place live financial orders
- transfer capital
- open or close financial positions
- acquire or dispose of assets
- bind the company to contracts
- represent itself as a human decision-maker
- bypass approval or governance controls

The distinction remains:

**ANALYSIS → RECOMMENDATION → HUMAN AUTHORIZATION → EXECUTION**

## Proprietary Means Proprietary

Do not weaken the private/proprietary requirement for convenience, demonstrations, portfolio visibility, community participation, or integration with third-party platforms.

Open-source dependencies may be used when appropriate, but the Institutional Intelligence Engine itself remains proprietary unless the human owner explicitly changes that decision.

External infrastructure is replaceable. The system's intellectual property, governance principles, reasoning architecture, and institutional learning loop are not to be treated as disposable implementation details.

## Required Behavior for Future Agents

When making a development recommendation, ask:

1. Does this preserve the private/proprietary status of the system?
2. Does this preserve human authority?
3. Does this preserve the Charter?
4. Could this expose proprietary source code, prompts, data, credentials, or decision logic?
5. Does this create an unintended path from analysis to autonomous execution?
6. If company integration is proposed, are the authorization and security boundaries explicit?

If the answer to any of these questions is unclear, stop and surface the uncertainty rather than silently assuming authorization.

## Relationship to CHARTER.md

`CHARTER.md` remains the constitutional governance layer and takes precedence over implementation details.

This document adds an explicit intellectual-property and future company-integration boundary. It does not weaken or replace the Charter.

**Private. Proprietary. Human-authorized. Company-ready only when explicitly approved.**
