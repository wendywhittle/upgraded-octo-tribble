# Taliesin Holdings LLC / AletheiaTelos Institutional Identity

## Canonical Relationship

**Taliesin Holdings LLC** is the institutional organization that operates the proprietary investment intelligence infrastructure.

**AletheiaTelos** is the proprietary Institutional Investment Intelligence System operated by Taliesin Holdings LLC.

Canonical hierarchy:

```text
TALIESIN HOLDINGS LLC
        ↓ operates
ALETHEIATELOS
        ↓
PROPRIETARY INSTITUTIONAL INVESTMENT INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM
```

## System Identity

- **Company:** Taliesin Holdings LLC
- **System:** AletheiaTelos
- **Category:** Institutional Investment Intelligence
- **Capital Engine:** AletheiaTelos Capital Engine
- **Asset Engine:** AletheiaTelos Asset Engine

AletheiaTelos is technology and institutional intelligence infrastructure. It is not itself the owner of capital, Investment Committee, trader, broker, principal, fund, or autonomous investment authority.

## Governing Boundaries

This identity layer does not change the system's architectural or governance boundaries.

AletheiaTelos remains:

- research and decision-intelligence infrastructure
- evidence-grounded and provenance-aware
- multi-perspective and adversarial
- independently risk-simulated where applicable
- human-governed at decision gates
- capable of NO-GO, NO DEAL, and INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE outcomes

AletheiaTelos does **not** autonomously:

- trade
- submit brokerage orders
- transfer capital
- mutate portfolios
- sign contracts
- close transactions
- acquire or dispose of assets
- exercise investment authority

The governing distinction remains:

```text
RECOMMENDATION
      ≠
READY FOR HUMAN AUTHORITY
      ≠
AUTHORIZED
      ≠
EXECUTED
```

The operating principle remains:

**AUTOMATION BETWEEN GATES. HUMAN AUTHORITY AT GATES.**

## Institutional Objects

The Decision Record remains the durable institutional object connecting evidence, reasoning, dissent, human decision, outcome, observation, attribution, and Epistemic Memory.

Epistemic Memory remains institutional history and learning. It does not become authority merely because it accumulates information.

## Naming Discipline

Use the following identity consistently in institutional-facing documentation and interfaces:

> **Taliesin Holdings LLC operates AletheiaTelos, its proprietary Institutional Investment Intelligence System.**

Do not describe AletheiaTelos as a legal entity, autonomous investment manager, public fund, broker, trading bot, or holder of independent investment authority.

Do not invent or imply regulatory status, clients, assets under management, performance, personnel, registrations, or other corporate claims that are not explicitly established elsewhere.

Internal technical module names may remain unchanged where renaming would create unnecessary implementation risk. AletheiaTelos remains the system identity.
