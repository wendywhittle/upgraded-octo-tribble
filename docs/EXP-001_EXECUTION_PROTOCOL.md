# EXP-001 Execution Protocol

The empirical run begins only after an authorized SPX/VIX/SKEW historical export or licensed feed has been obtained.

The dataset must preserve acquisition metadata, point-in-time availability, source/version identity, methodology version, and an immutable fingerprint. Synthetic fixtures are mechanics-only and are never empirical evidence.

The locked experiment then constructs five-day features and future drawdown labels, runs the preregistered baseline and SKEW-augmented models, reports Brier score, log loss, and AUC, and sends the evidence through Skeptic/Contrarian review.

AletheiaTelos remains research-only: no brokerage connectivity, live orders, trading credentials, capital transfer, or portfolio mutation. Consequential decisions require human authorization.
