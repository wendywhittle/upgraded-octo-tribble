# Experiment #001 — SKEW Incremental Information

## Research question

Does SKEW contain incremental information about subsequent SPX drawdowns after controlling for recent SPX behavior and VIX?

## Pre-registered hypotheses

- **H1:** SKEW adds incremental information after controlling for recent SPX behavior and VIX.
- **H0:** SKEW adds no meaningful incremental information.

## Locked design

- Forecast horizon: **5 trading days**.
- Event definition: future maximum SPX drawdown at or below **-3%** over the forecast horizon.
- Baseline predictors: 5-day SPX return, VIX level, 5-day VIX change.
- Incremental predictors: SKEW level and 5-day SKEW change.
- Evaluation: chronological 70/30 train/test split; no random shuffling.
- Primary comparison: out-of-sample Brier score and log loss; AUC is reported as a secondary discrimination metric.
- Data requirement: every feature must be point-in-time valid at the observation timestamp. No future revisions or look-ahead features are permitted.

## Interpretation rule

A lower augmented Brier/log-loss score or higher augmented AUC is evidence of incremental predictive value, but does not by itself establish economic significance, stability across regimes, or tradability. Those questions require additional pre-specified tests.

The implementation reports the incremental metric differences without automatically declaring H1 true.

## Governance

This is a research experiment. It cannot place orders, connect to a brokerage, transfer capital, or mutate portfolio state. Any later investment decision remains a human decision.

## Data status

The repository implementation currently contains only a deterministic synthetic fixture for testing the evaluation mechanics. **Synthetic data is not evidence for H1.** The next step is to populate the experiment with historical point-in-time SPX, VIX, and CBOE SKEW observations, preserve source provenance, and run the locked evaluation unchanged.
